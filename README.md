# Pizza Sales Data Analysis
Data-driven dashboard to analyze pizza sales using Power BI and SQL – with actionable business insights.

## Project Overview

Analyzed over 48,000 rows of pizza sales data using **SQL** to uncover actionable business insights, identify peak sales hours, top-performing products, and revenue trends. Developed an interactive dashboard in **Power BI** featuring 15+ visuals to track key performance indicators (KPIs) such as sales by category, time-based performance, and customer preferences.

## Features

### SQL-based Data Exploration
- Identified KPIs including total revenue, average order value, order size distribution, and more.
- Performed in-depth analysis of sales patterns by time (hourly, daily, monthly) and by product (category, size, topping).

### Dynamic Power BI Dashboard
- Over 15 interactive visuals: bar charts, line graphs, heatmaps, pie charts, and more.
- Visualizes sales performance by pizza type, category, and size.
- Shows trend analysis by month, day, and hour.
- Highlights best and worst selling products.
- Displays customer ordering behavior and preferences.

## Key Insights
- Peak sales occur during lunch hours (12–1 PM), and on Fridays & Saturdays.
- Large and Classic pizzas contribute most to revenue.
- Majority of orders are small (1–2 pizzas per order).
- Some menu items significantly outperform others.

## Technology Used
- **MS Excel**
- **SQL** (Data Extraction, Transformation, and Analysis)
- **Power BI** (Dashboard Development)
